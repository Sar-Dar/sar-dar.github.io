About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Technology at Xinjiang University, supervised by Prof. Hao Huang. I am also a research intern at the Speech Processing and Machine Intelligence (SPMI) Lab at Tsinghua University, supervised by Prof. Zhijian Ou.

My research focuses on data-efficient multilingual and crosslingual automatic speech recognition. I am particularly interested in phoneme-based modeling and how shared speech representations can help build recognition systems for low-resource languages.

My work includes Whistle, which uses weak phonetic supervision, and JSA-SPG, a pronunciation-lexicon-free training approach. I am also a core contributor to the CAT speech recognition toolkit.

Research Area

I study methods that make speech recognition more accessible across languages, particularly when transcribed data and pronunciation lexicons are limited.

  • Multilingual, crosslingual and low-resource speech recognition
  • Phoneme-based modeling and speech-language interfaces
  • Weakly supervised and lexicon-free learning
  • Speech foundation models and LLM-based speech recognition
